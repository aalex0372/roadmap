## Casino decision — SETTLED: **NO**, permanently `DZ-43`

> This is a **closed** decision, recorded here so it is not relitigated. It is not reopened without gambling counsel confirming a license that legally reaches the audience *(standing rule #6)*.

**Verdict: NO on any surface, permanently — and the "walled-off cash-casino entity later" escape hatch is a licensing dead-end for the US/UK/EU audience, not a post-beta option.**

Two facts settle it:

1. **Near-term upside ≈ $0.** Casino revenue = affiliate-rate × referred volume (or sponsorship priced on reach) — both strictly downstream of an *installed audience*. Dropzona has ~0 activated streamers today. The headline figures (~$200k/mo offers, ~10% affiliate share) are mature-network / top-streamer numbers. **There is no cheque to resist.** Worse, surfacing a casino *during* the build destroys the very engine that would one day create the audience.
2. **The escape hatch is a licensing dead-end money cannot fix.** The affordable offshore license (Curaçao/Anjouan) is **geo-blocked out of the US/UK/EU** — exactly the CS2 audience; the license that reaches them (US state-by-state + UKGC) runs to **millions** and is effectively closed to a skin/crypto casino; serving them unlicensed is **criminally exposed** (worst case NY, where the AG is litigating skins-as-gambling); and the recurring **AML/MLRO/audit apparatus (~$50k+/yr)** is unstaffable by a two-person team.

| Option | Near-term upside | Platform risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stay clean (baseline)** | Keeps both platform dependencies + the only moat; ships beta fastest; fundable & acquirable on merit; rides the tailwind | **Low but non-zero** baseline tail-risk | ✅ **Chosen** |
| Walled-off NewCo, cash-casino B2B | **~$0 realizable** (upside = *f*(installed base ≈ 0)) | Licensing dead-end — no affordable structure legally reaches the audience | ❌ Dead-end |
| Casino on the product surface | Fastest cash today | **Near-certain death** — Twitch OAuth revoked + all participating streamers banned + Steam API revoked | ❌ No mitigation exists |

**Why on-surface is existential (settled):** Twitch's Community Guidelines and Jan-2026 Enforcement Notes prohibit *"the promotion or sponsorship of skins gambling, such as for CSGO skins"* **by name** — a tool that surfaces a skin casino on-stream (co-brand, affiliate link, referral code) carries that promotional act onto every streamer's channel. Valve can revoke Steam access *"at any time for any reason, without notice"* and has precedent for banning on *association* alone. **Casino money is rent, not equity** — a line Valve or Twitch can cut the moment we antagonize them.

**Roadmap consequence:** casino is `status/parked` with no owner and no milestone (`DZ-43`). The decisive move is boring on purpose — **ship the beta, close the two P0 money-loss items on the [build track](./backend-money-track.md), land the founding clip.**
